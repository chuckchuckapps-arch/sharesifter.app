How it works

From filter to insight in seconds

ShareSifter is built around a simple workflow — screen, explore, research.

1

Set your filters

Choose a built-in strategy or dial in your own criteria: market cap tier, maximum P/E ratio, and minimum daily price change.

2

See the results

The screener fetches live data and surfaces only the stocks that meet your criteria. Sort by price, change, market cap, or symbol.

3

Tap for details

Open any stock for a full detail view: price chart, key financials, and sector. Add to your portfolio with one tap.

4

Research with AI

On supported devices, generate an on-device AI summary that contextualizes the stock's valuation, growth story, and risks.
